A QUESTION WORTH ASKING

Do you remember the mango that actually tasted like a mango?

Your grandparents might remember food grown close to home, harvested in season and connected to the soil it came from.

Today, much of our food travels through long supply chains before reaching our kitchens. It can be difficult to know where it was grown, which practices were followed or how it was handled after harvest.

We have confused appearance with the whole story of food.

At Aarogya Bhoomi, we want families to reconnect with the people, soil and seasons behind what they eat.

Rather than making broad claims about all market produce, we invite you to visit our farm, understand our growing practices and follow the journey from seed to harvest.

Our belief is simple: food deserves care, transparency and a closer relationship between the people who grow it and the people who eat it.

Three Problems. One root.

The Soil is Dying

What grows in dead soil is not food — it is the shape of food. It looks right. It weighs right. But the nutrition is gone. And what we lose in the soil, we pay for in our bodies.

Soil and Water Need Care

Farming choices can affect soil structure, water quality and biodiversity. We focus on practices that care for the living systems our food depends on.

Our Children Inherit Our Choices

The way we grow food shapes the soil and environment future generations depend on. We want children to learn where food comes from and why caring for nature matters.

HOW WE GROW

Farming the ancient way, for the modern family.

🌿

Indigenous Seeds

We select indigenous and open-pollinated seeds suited to local conditions, and work to preserve traditional varieties through cultivation and seed saving.

Desi Cow as Pillar

We use compost, cow-based preparations and other natural farm formulations as part of our soil-care practices. We avoid synthetic fertilisers.

Pest Management, not Killing

We prioritise preventive and ecological pest-management practices, including natural formulations and habitat-based approaches, while respecting the role of biodiversity on the farm.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF PARTNERSHIP

Why does the farmer carry this alone?

Think about the person who grew what you ate today. He was in the field before the sun rose. He watched the sky nervously when the monsoon was late. He took loans to buy seeds - more loans when the harvest didn't cover the last ones.

And at the end of all that - he got paid less than what it cost him to grow.

What if this didn't have to be his burden alone?

What if the people who eat the food also shared the responsibility of growing it? Not as charity. Not as sympathy. As equals.

This is the Co-Farmer Model

You share the cost of growing food through a dedicated family plot. Our farm team tends the land using natural farming practices. Visit the farm, watch seeds go into the ground, take part in harvesting and let your children experience the soil. Produce can be collected at the farm; optional weekly home delivery is available at an additional charge.

When the harvest is good, we celebrate together. When it isn't - we bear the loss together.

We want farming to become a dignified career - chosen with pride, not forced by circumstance. Every family that joins makes the farmer's path a little less lonely. Makes the soil a little more alive.

Co-farmer Plans

Choose your depth of connection

A Dedicated Family Plot. Seasonal Food. A Connection with Nature.

Give your family the joy of growing, nurturing and harvesting their own food. Become a co-farmer at Aarogya Bhoomi and experience the beauty of natural farming, one season at a time.

Most farmers don't grow food naturally-not because they don't want to, but because they can't afford the risk. Natural farming requires time, patience, and most importantly - a guaranteed buyer for the season.

Without that assurance, farmers are forced to depend on chemicals for faster, predictable yields.

At Aarogya Bhoomi, your seasonal commitment helps support a more planned relationship between the farm and the families who participate in it.

You don't just receive vegetables. You witness your food growing, understand the process, and become a co-farmer in the journey.

Harvest quantities and varieties are seasonal and indicative, not guaranteed. Weekly home delivery is optional and charged separately at ₹250 per week.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you want to know before you join.

As a Co-Farmer, your family gets a dedicated rental plot at Aarogya Bhoomi. Our farm team prepares and tends the land using natural farming practices, while you get to participate in growing, learning and harvesting. You also receive an equal family share of produce from our community plantations as it matures, along with access to farm activities and our members' community. It is about becoming part of the farming journey, not simply purchasing vegetables.
Aarogya Bhoomi is located on Sohna Road, Gurgaon, Haryana. Members can visit the farm on their own or during scheduled farm activities and events. We share farm updates regularly through our members' WhatsApp group.
We offer four Co-Farmer memberships: Beej: 50-yard plot for 3 months, ideal for 2–3 family members. Beej Plus: 50-yard plot for 6 months, ideal for 2–3 family members. Fasal: 100-yard plot for 3 months, ideal for 4–5 family members. Fasal Plus: 100-yard plot for 6 months, ideal for 4–5 family members. Both Plus plans include a waived plot setup fee. Beej Plus receives a 5% membership discount (₹1,200), bringing the total to ₹22,800. Fasal Plus receives a special 10% membership discount (₹3,900), bringing the total to ₹35,100. Fasal Plus is marked as our Popular Choice.
The 3-month Beej membership has a one-time setup fee of ₹1,500 for a 50-yard plot. The 3-month Fasal membership has a one-time setup fee of ₹2,000 for a 100-yard plot. The setup fee covers soil preparation, farming beds, a basic tool kit, your family's plot signboard and a welcome walk. For both six-month Plus memberships, the setup fee is completely waived.
